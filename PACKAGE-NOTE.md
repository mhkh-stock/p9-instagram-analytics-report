# P9 — Package Note (Fiverr-ready)

**Gig title:** I will audit your Instagram business page and tell you exactly what to post next

**What the client gets:** a short PDF report, not a data dump — a
90-day (or 30-day) audit of their posts with reach and engagement
rate by format, their top posts, and a prioritised action list where
every recommendation traces to a computed number. The client stops
guessing what to post and gets one page of "do this first".

## Tiers (Hashir's band)

| Tier | Price | Delivery | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $20 | 2 days | 30-day audit: headline KPIs + engagement by post type + top-5 posts + 5 actions, PDF report |
| Standard | $65 | 3 days | 90-day audit, weekly trend chart, weekday/best-time analysis, hashtag notes, 10 prioritised actions, post-level data sheet |
| Premium | $140 | 5 days | 90-day audit + competitor-format comparison (3 competitor pages, public metrics only), 30-day content calendar built from the findings, reusable audit script for the client's own future exports, 1 revision round |

**What's NOT included:** posting or managing the account (that's a
management service, quoted separately); buying followers or
engagement (never offered); ad campaign management (quoted on
seeing the ad account).

**Delivery time note:** counts from the day the client uploads their
Instagram Insights export, not from order placement.

**Proof this service works:** this repo's sample — fictional
DesiBites PK, 36 posts over 90 days, 178,122 reach, 11.53% overall
engagement rate, carousels beating Reels on engagement rate
(12.77% vs 11.25%), followers 8,420 → 9,581, 19 automated checks
passing. The sample report is the demo the buyer sees before
ordering.
